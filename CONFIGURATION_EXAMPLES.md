## How to Use These Examples

1. Go to `http://localhost:3000/admin`
2. Update the color fields with values from your chosen theme
3. Update messages
4. Click "Save Theme"
5. Colors change instantly on main page!

## Create Your Own Combination

**Steps:**
1. Pick your girlfriend's favorite colors
2. Use a color picker: https://htmlcolorcodes.com
3. Test combinations in the admin panel
4. Save when you like it

## Color Picking Tips

- **Bright & Fun**: Use saturated colors (pure reds, bright pinks, vibrant blues)
- **Romantic**: Use soft pinks, purples, reds
- **Elegant**: Use gold, silver, blacks together
- **Fresh**: Use greens, blues, teals
- **Warm**: Use oranges, corals, peaches

## Text Color Tips

- Use **dark text** (`#000000`, `#333333`) on **light backgrounds**
- Use **light text** (`#ffffff`, `#f5f5f5`) on **dark backgrounds**
- Ensure good contrast for readability

## Font Pairing Ideas

The site uses "Segoe UI" but you can customize by editing CSS:

- **Romantic**: `'Georgia', serif`
- **Modern**: `'Arial', sans-serif`
- **Fun**: `'Comic Sans MS', cursive`
- **Elegant**: `'Trebuchet MS', sans-serif`

## Message Writing Tips

**Main Message:**
- Keep it short (under 50 characters)
- Use her name or nickname
- Examples:
  - "Happy Birthday, [Name]!"
  - "You're Amazing, [Name]!"
  - "Happy Birthday to My Love"

**Sub Message:**
- Describe her in one line
- Examples:
  - "Beautiful inside and out"
  - "The best person I know"
  - "My everything and so much more"

**Personal Message:**
- Can be longer (200+ characters)
- Be specific about what you love about her
- Share memories or inside jokes
- Express your feelings
- Make it personal and heartfelt

## Photo Tips

**Best photos to include:**
- Clear face photos
- Couple photos
- Candid/natural moments
- Her hobbies/interests
- Favorite memories together

**Technical tips:**
- Use high-res photos for better quality
- Recommended size: at least 1080x1080px
- Formats: JPG, PNG both work great
- File size: Under 5MB each
